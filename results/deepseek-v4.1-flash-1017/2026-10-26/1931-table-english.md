*Most of the information on total farm families and total cultivated area per hsien is from data published under the direction of C. C. Chang of the former Statistical Bureau of the Legislative Yuan and from data collected in connection with the 1931 flood survey of The University of Nanking. Other sources were chiefly provincial government figures.
The percentages of cultivated area flooded are chiefly from estimates obtained from the 1931 flood survey of The University of Nanking and from planimetered data of land area flooded. Percentages from these two sources were checked with those obtained under the direction of C. C. Chang of the Directorate of Statistics and those reported by Magistrates to the National Flood Relief Commission, and with maps of area flooded.
For some hsien different sources gave great variation in the number of farm families and the amount of cultivated area. Determination of the more reasonable number was reached in part by computation of the density of population per square mile of cultivated area. Even the best figures give densities of great variation and often appear to be too high or too low. The average density of 1,160 persons per square mile of cultivated area, for the entire area of the 131 hsien appears to be fairly reasonable as it checks with population densities previously obtained by the Department of Agricultural Economics, University of Nanking.
In cases of individual hsien, however, there may be serious underestimates of either number of families or of amount of cultivated area. For various reasons the amount of cultivated area is likely to be understated by at least 10-20 per cent.
